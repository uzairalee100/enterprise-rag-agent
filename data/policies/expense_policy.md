# Expense Reimbursement Policy

## Scope
This policy applies to all full-time and part-time employees seeking reimbursement for business-related expenses.

## Eligible Expenses
- Travel: economy-class flights, standard rail fares, and mid-tier hotel accommodation for approved business trips.
- Meals: up to $75 per day while traveling on approved business, itemized receipts required above $25.
- Client entertainment: up to $150 per event, requires manager pre-approval and a list of attendees.
- Home office equipment: up to $500 per calendar year for remote employees, covering monitors, keyboards, and chairs.
- Professional development: conference fees and certification exams up to $2,000 per year, subject to manager approval.

## Non-Reimbursable Expenses
- First-class or business-class airfare without VP-level approval.
- Alcohol purchased outside of approved client entertainment.
- Personal entertainment, spa services, or in-room movies.
- Traffic or parking fines.

## Submission Process
1. Submit expense reports through the Expense Portal within 30 days of the expense date.
2. Attach itemized receipts for any single expense over $25.
3. Reports are routed to the employee's direct manager for approval.
4. Approved reports are reimbursed via payroll within 2 pay cycles.

## Escalation
Expenses that exceed policy limits require written justification and approval from a Director or above. Repeated policy violations may result in a review by HR.
