# IT Security and Acceptable Use Policy

## Scope
Applies to all employees, contractors, and vendors with access to company systems, devices, or data.

## Account Security
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for all corporate accounts, including email, VPN, and internal tools.
- Passwords must be at least 14 characters and rotated every 180 days.
- Shared accounts are prohibited except for designated service accounts approved by IT Security.

## Device Management
- All company-issued laptops must have disk encryption and endpoint detection software enabled before first use.
- Personal devices used for email or chat access must be enrolled in the Mobile Device Management (MDM) program.
- Lost or stolen devices must be reported to IT Security within 1 hour of discovery.

## Data Classification
- Public: information approved for external release.
- Internal: information for employee use only, not for external sharing.
- Confidential: sensitive business data such as financial reports, source code, and customer contracts.
- Restricted: highly sensitive data such as personal identifiable information (PII), health records, and payment card data. Restricted data requires encryption at rest and in transit.

## Incident Reporting
- Suspected phishing emails must be reported using the "Report Phishing" button in the email client.
- Security incidents involving Confidential or Restricted data must be reported to security@company.com within 24 hours.
- Employees who fail to report a known incident may be subject to disciplinary action.

## Third-Party and AI Tool Usage
- Use of external AI tools with Confidential or Restricted data requires prior approval from IT Security and Legal.
- Approved AI tools are listed in the internal Tool Catalog; unlisted tools may not be used with company data.
