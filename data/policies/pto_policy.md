# Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy

## Accrual
- Full-time employees accrue 1.75 days of PTO per month, totaling 21 days per year.
- Employees with 5+ years of tenure accrue 2.5 days per month, totaling 30 days per year.
- PTO accrual begins on an employee's start date and is prorated for the first partial month.

## Carryover
- Up to 5 unused PTO days may be carried over into the next calendar year.
- Any PTO balance beyond 5 days is forfeited on December 31st unless local law requires otherwise.

## Requesting Time Off
1. Submit PTO requests through the HR Portal at least 5 business days in advance for planned absences.
2. Manager approval is required before time off is considered confirmed.
3. Requests for more than 10 consecutive PTO days require Director approval and a coverage plan.

## Sick Leave
- Sick leave is tracked separately from PTO and is not capped on a per-request basis.
- Absences longer than 5 consecutive days require a doctor's note submitted to HR.

## Holidays
- The company observes 10 paid public holidays per year, published annually on the HR Portal.
- Employees required to work on a published holiday receive a floating holiday to use within 90 days.

## Unused PTO at Termination
- Unused, accrued PTO is paid out at termination in jurisdictions where required by law.
